A little about me

I am a woman, friend, daughter, mother, wife, educator, lifelong learner, and now a coach, and I am ready to support you on your journey.

My life has brought me many changes—from raising a child on my own and marrying later, to moving through menopause and starting a fresh career. I know the power of trusting my inner voice. Now, I want to help other women discover their own answers and embrace their personal growth.

As an educator, I have worked with people of all ages for 30 years, and helped them grow by discovering their own strengths and voices. I have collaborated with colleagues and parents to facilitate students' development.

I have a genuine passion for helping women of any age find their voice and path.

I believe that we all have the answers within us, and we just need to listen.
